# Brooklyn 99 :cop:

**Genre:** Workplace Comedy, Sitcom, and Police Procedural  
**Created By:** Dan Goor and Michael Schur  

> "Every time someone steps up and says who they are, the world becomes a better, more interesting place."

*My favorite line from the entire show!* :heart_eyes:

## Main Characters
* **Jake Peralta** – Talented but childish detective
* **Amy Santiago** – Highly organized and ambitious detective
* **Captain Raymond Holt** – Strict, wise, and stoic captain
* **Terry Jeffords** – Devoted sergeant who loves yogurt
* **Rosa Diaz** – Tough and private detective
* **Charles Boyle** – Loyal best friend and food enthusiast
* **Hitchcock & Scully** – The precinct's most iconic, food-obsessed lazy duo who somehow always get the job done

## Favorite Moments
- Jake proposing to Amy
- Halloween Heists
- Gina leaving everyone their "*Gina Moments*"

## Top Reasons to Watch
